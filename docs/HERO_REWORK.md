# Hero rework

Status: Stages 1–3 are implemented. Nothing has been tested in the running game; checks so far are compilation, Mono JIT of every method (`tools/verify.sh`), unit tests (`tests/HeroKindsTests.cs`) and a smoke test on real simulation objects (`tools/Verify/HeroSmoke.cs`).

## Kinds (Stage 1)
The kind follows the vanilla hero class (`HeroDefinition.Class`):

| Class (ship design) | Kind |
|---|---|
| `HeroClassAdmiral` (Attacker) | Admiral |
| `HeroClassAdventurer` (Explorer) | Admiral — its tree is almost all ship skills |
| `HeroClassAdministrator` (Defender) | Governor |
| `HeroClassCorporate` (Supporter) | Senator |

A hero of an unknown (modded) class falls back to `GUID % 3`. AI-ruler heroes are not touched. Trees that keep no useful skill for the kind are dropped, so a kind only gets branches that exist for it.
The mapping lives in `HeroKinds.TryKindOfClass`.
The skill descriptors a hero provides are filtered by channel: Admiral → Ship, Governor → System, Senator → Senate. The Hero channel is kept for all.
Skill trees are rebuilt per kind (skills with no effect for the kind are dropped, stage thresholds are clamped to the points that remain).
Assigning a hero to the wrong job (`CheckHeroAssignableToFleet/ToSystem`, `Senator.UpdateRepresentativeHero`) is refused.
The hero title shows the kind (`GuiHero.get_Title`).

## Ultimate and automatic growth (Stage 2), admirals and governors only
* A hero's *ultimate* is any skill of the final stage (maximum `RequiredLevel`) of any of his ORIGINAL trees (`HeroDefinition.GetSkillTrees()`), because after kind filtering the final skill of a tree often belongs to another channel.
* The level at which it is taken is recorded (`HeroRework.ultimates`). From then on `Hero.availableSkillDefinitionNames` is emptied: the hero offers no more skills, specialization is fixed.
* Every hero level above that gives every learned skill an extra `100 / ultimateLevel` percent of its effect: scaled copies of the skill descriptors are appended to the lists returned by `Hero.Get*SkillDescriptors`.
  Scaling: Addition/Subtraction/Percent are linear, Multiplication scales the excess over 1, Binary(property × constant) scales the constant; other operations (Force, Min, Max…) and property-reference values are not scaled.
  Copies are new objects with fresh `ModifierId`, non-serializable, cached by (descriptor, bonus).
* A turn pass (`HeroRework.PerTurn`, from `CouncilGame.ClientBeginTurn`) refreshes a hero's effects (`Hero.OnSkillsUpdated`) when his level changed.
* Dummy heroes (skill editing window copies) never record an ultimate: only the entity registered in `IGameEntityRepositoryService` does.
* Persistence: the registry (`guid:level,…`) is appended to the `CouncilState` attribute after `@@HR@@`; old saves load with an empty registry (a hero already holding an ultimate is fixed at his current level on the first turn pass).

## Senators and the dictatorship (Stage 3)
* **Level cap.** A senator hero (not the dictator) stops at hero level `HeroKinds.SenatorLevelMax` = 8 (seven level-ups). Done by giving the hero a clone of his `LevelUpRuleDefinition` with `LevelMax = 8` at the start of `Hero.UpdateLevel(bool)` (`HeroRework.BeforeUpdateLevel`). Heroes already above the cap keep their level.
* **Official ideology** is the politics of the prime senator (`Senate.MainPolitics`); the prime senator is the one with the highest `Support`.
* **Dictatorship** is detected by the senate simulation tags `GovernmentDictatorship` / `GovernmentDictatorshipCravers` (the same ones `Senate.OnLoad` uses).
  * When a dictatorship starts, its leader is the prime senator at that moment. This is the "chosen in advance" senator: the player prepares him with the elections before switching. The senate is trimmed to this one senator (`RemoveSenator`).
  * While the leader rules, the ideology is locked against external factors: `Senate.ParseElectionResult` (start hook `LockElection`) rewrites the candidate list to the leader only, and `Senate.ServerTriggerRebellion` (start hook `BlockRebellion`) does nothing, so unrest and rebellion-causing events cannot overthrow the government and replace the ideology. System-level riots still happen.
  * The player is the only one who can end it: by changing the government (abdication, `OrderSwapGovernment`) or when the government changes for another reason the turn pass forgets the record (`UpdateDictatorships`).
  * The record (`empireIndex=politics=senatorGuid`) is persisted after the ultimates, split by `;`.
* **Dictator hero** = the representative hero of the leader senator (a senator kind hero, class Corporate). He has no level cap (only the vanilla rule), and the same ultimate rule as admirals/governors: after his ultimate no new skills are offered, every learned skill gets `+DictatorUltimateBoost` (x2.75 effect, "2 levels become 5–6") plus the per-level growth `100 / ultimateLevel` percent. The title shows `[Диктатор]`.

## Skill content (Stage 4)
All skills are generated at run time from the live skill database (`HeroContent.cs`, pure data in `HeroSkillData.cs`) and registered in the game databases together with `GuiElement`s and localization (`CustomHackingHelper.RegisterLoc`), so no game XML is shipped.

* *(Stage 4 as first built; superseded by the section "Redesign of all trees" above, only the registration mechanism still applies.)*
* **Roles** (code in the skill name): `@A` admiral, `@G` governor, `@S` senator, `@D` dictator. Every skill of every vanilla tree exists in all four versions: `<VanillaName>@A` etc.
* **Conversion.** Each vanilla modifier is classified into a *flavor* (offense, defense, mobility, sustain, upkeep, production, growth, science, money, influence, happiness, manpower, deposit, experience, diplomacy, council votes) with a magnitude (1 = a typical second-level skill). The effects of the role's own channel are kept; the effects of the other channels become the flavor's effect in the role's channel, so no skill is wasted: "+10 production / +30% damage" gives the governor "+10 production / +0.2 garrison health" and the admiral "+20% hull plating / +30% damage". Penalties, forced values and non-numeric modifiers are not converted; a level that would stay empty gets a modest effect by its mastery.
* **Senator version:** one level (the strongest vanilla level), stage thresholds halved (0/2/4/6 points: seven level-ups reach the last stage), effects empire-wide through the senate channel.
* **Dictator version:** senate channel, 3 levels for two-level skills, effects x`DictatorPower` (1.6), thresholds x1.5 (0/6/12/18).
* **Trees of a hero** (`BuildHeroTrees`): his race tree and class tree with the skills of his role (an admiral Sophon and a governor Sophon have different Sophon trees), and the **party tree** (`HRTreeParty0N@code`) instead of "Universal". Party themes: 1 Industrialists (production, money, manpower), 2 Scientists (science, experience, influence), 3 Pacifists (happiness, **council votes**, growth), 4 Ecologists (growth, deposits, sustain), 5 Religious (happiness, influence, **council votes**), 6 Militarists (offense, defense, manpower). Seven skills per tree: stage 0: A, B; stage 1: C, A+B; stage 2: B+C, A+C; stage 3: ultimate A+B+C.
* **Dictator** gets the senate version of his race tree, the dictator version of his party tree and a **unique faction tree** (`HRTreeDict<Faction>`) instead of the class tree. Faction themes: Sophons science/food/influence, Cravers war/manpower/industry, Terrans industry/money/influence, Venetians money/influence/council votes, Vampirilis influence/offense/sustain, Time Lords science/mobility/experience, Unfallen defense/food/happiness, Endless science/influence/industry, and so on; the tree ultimate also gives happiness and manpower. A hero who becomes or stops being the dictator gets his skills recomputed.
* **Ultimate** = a skill of the last stage of the hero's *own* (new) trees.
* `HeroSkill.Load` (start) calls `HeroRework.EnsureContent()` so saves find the skills by name.
* **Council votes** (`Flavor.Votes`): senator and dictator skills of this flavor have no senate modifier; the Galactic Council reads the learned skills (see `docs/GALACTIC_COUNCIL.md`). Governors and admirals get influence instead.
* Magnitudes are balance guesses in `HeroSkillData.cs` (target tables and `Classify` reference values): tune there.

## Redesign of all trees (2026-10-24) - replaces the "conversion" of Stage 4

The generated "vanilla skill converted by flavor" trees were replaced by trees written by hand. Code: `HeroTreeDesign.cs` (library of ideas, "concepts": the effect, its unit, steps per tier, the game properties it changes) and `HeroTreeSpecs.cs` (one line per tree and role: `T1:..;T2:..;T3:..;U:..`, the ultimate titles, builders, texts, votes by skill name). `HeroContent.cs` registers every tree and skill (`RegisterDesigned`), `BuildHeroTrees` picks them by `TreeKeyOfVanilla`.

* **Which trees:** 6 party trees, 13 race trees, 4 class trees (a class tree exists only for the roles that have such heroes: Admiral/Adventurer -> A, Administrator -> G, Corporate -> S) and 14 dictator faction trees; each for the roles A, G, S, D (94 trees, 445 skills). Names: skill `HR<key>_<n>@<role>` (`HRDict<Faction>_<n>` has no role suffix), tree `HRTree<key>@<role>`. A dictator gets the senate trees of his party and race and his faction tree instead of the class tree. Vanilla trees are no longer used; personal skills of heroes are untouched.
* **Rules of design:** the theme decides the effects (Industrialists have no upkeep skills, Pacifists no attack skills); one idea is never repeated in a tree (tests check concepts and idea groups; only the ultimate may repeat); if there are too few distinct ideas the tree has fewer skills with more levels; only whole numbers are shown (no 8.4%); nothing touches the ship manpower reserve or ship crew restoration.
* **Levels:** A/G skills have 5 levels (view / movement range 3-4 because of the range limits), the senator 3, the dictator 5; level N is N x the step of its tier (steps grow per tier); a tree has three tiers with one to three skills each and one single-level ultimate that combines four effects. Thresholds: A/G 0/4/8/12, senator 0/2/4/6, dictator 0/6/12/18. `HeroKinds.DictatorUltimateBoost` is now 0.
* **Special ultimates** (`HeroSpecials.cs`, hook: end of `GameClient.ApplyDamage(...)`, i.e. **automatically resolved battles only**; manual tactical battles are not covered): Salvage (Dust / science / influence per destroyed enemy CP), Cravers' Taking Captives (one citizen of the defeated faction appears in the capital; pirates and minor factions give none), Terrans' Triumph (+10 happiness in every system for 5 turns + 1 turn per 10 CP destroyed; the state is saved with the hero state after a `~`).
* **Property mapping is by meaning and unverified in the game:** hull armor `ShipHullPlatingAbsorptionPercent`, out-of-battle repair `FlatHealthRegeneration`, self-repair `HealthRegenerationRatio`, evasion `Evasion`, weapon damage by type `ShipDamage*`, rate of fire = weapon cooldown, crit `ShipCriticalHitChance`, accuracy `ShipAccuracy` (probably no effect in automatic battles), shield health `MaximumShield`, shield reload `ShieldReloadPerPhase`, crew kill / crew loss `ManpowerDamageOverTime(Defense)`, siege `SiegePower`, upkeep `GarrisonMoneyUpkeep`.
* **Dropped:** "crew replenishment outside battle" of the Ecologists (it touches ship crew restoration, which must stay untouched).
* Listings: see below (regenerated from this code).

## Playtest follow-ups (2026-10-24)
* **Dictator hero is given.** While an empire (human or AI) has a dictatorship (including a start with one and the Cravers' authoritarian rule), `UpdateDictatorships` calls `SyncDictatorHero`: if the empire has no hero whose definition name starts with `HeroDefinitionGCDictator_`, the order is posted (client of the player for a human, server for an AI empire) as a normal `OrderRecruitHero` (level 1, once per 3 turns at most). When the dictatorship ends, or the leader senator has another politics, the hero is removed with `DepartmentOfEducation.RemoveHero` (on every client, the representative links of the senators are cleared first and recomputed after).
  * Definitions are generated at run time, one per (faction, politics): hidden, `IgnoreInstanceNumber`, class/ship/level rule of a vanilla Corporate hero, affinity of a hero of the faction (fallback `Other` = any Corporate hero), race tree of that faction hero, politics = the politics of the leader, so `Senator.UpdateRepresentative` picks him (a dictator definition always wins over a regular hero of the same party). GUI element copied from the template, title "Диктатор: <фракция>".
  * Registered by `HeroRework.EnsureDictatorDefinitions` at the start of `DepartmentOfEducation.SerializeElements` (before a save reads its heroes) and again on demand.
  * The faction of the empire is read from `Faction.Name`, `GetAffinityMappingName()` and `GetAffinityGameplayName()` through `HeroFlavors.FactionKey`; an unknown faction uses the `Other` set (the log line `[HeroRework] dictator hero definitions: N` is written once per registration).
* **No skill points after the ultimate.** `Hero.get_AvailableSkillPoints` is wrapped by `HeroRework.AdjustAvailablePoints`: 0 for a hero whose ultimate is registered (admiral, governor, dictator, senator with ultimate). This removes the "assign skills" prompt, the level-up window and the points counter; the per-level percentage growth of the learned skills is unchanged.
* **Passive experience of senators.** Each turn every senator kind hero of every empire gets `NextLevelRequestedExperience / 10` (`/ 6` for the dictator) through `Hero.SetExperience`; the level cap (8 for senators) and the level-up rule decide whether it counts. Constants: `HeroKinds.SenatorXpTurnsPerLevel`, `DictatorXpTurnsPerLevel`.

## Academy offer: choose the kind first ("variant 2b", 2026-10-25)
The vanilla game answers a filled Academy gauge with a random trio (`Academy.PickHeroesForRecruitment` → `OrderOfferHeroChoice` → the recruitment notification with three cards). The mod adds one question for the local player - **which kind of hero** - and reshapes that trio into **two candidates of the picked kind** (files `src/GalacticCouncil/HeroAcademyOffer.cs`, `HeroAcademy.cs`, `OrderAcademyHeroKind.cs`):
* **Dialog.** The start of `HeroRecruitmentNotificationWindow.Refresh` calls `HeroRework.OnRecruitNoteRefresh`: a fresh untouched vanilla trio (3 references) of the local human empire triggers a native `GuiManager.ShowMessage` with three buttons - Адмирал / Губернатор / Сенатор (`Ok` / `Cancel` / `Alternative`). ESC or a right click closes it silently: the vanilla trio stays, nothing is sent. The same trio (the same `HeroChoice` object) is never asked twice; a second trio while a modal is visible keeps the vanilla flow.
* **Dictatorship warning** (verbatim wording): picking Сенатор under a Dictatorship/Authoritarian government (`HeroRework.IsDictatorship(SenateOf(empire))`, tags `GovernmentDictatorship` / `GovernmentDictatorshipCravers`) shows a second message box (`MessageBoxType.WARNING`): «Внимание, ваш политический строй - Диктатура! …» with the buttons «Я понимаю, что делаю, дайте мне выбрать нового героя роли Сенатора!» (→ the senator pair) and «Ой... Я ошибся. Пожалуйста, дайте мне выбрать между Адмиралом и Наместником» (→ an Admiral/Governor dialog). Follow-up dialogs are shown one frame later (`NextFrame` coroutine) because the message box closes itself right after the callback.
* **Order.** The answer dismisses the trio notification (`IGuiNotificationService.DismissGuiNotification`) and posts `OrderAcademyHeroKind(empireIndex, gaugeIndex, kind)` (`DepartmentOfEducation/OrderAcademyHeroKind`, registered as `[OrderProcessor(typeof(OrderAcademyHeroKind), "AcademyHeroKind")]` on `DepartmentOfEducation`, methods cloned from the Treasury pattern). AI empires never send it - they keep the vanilla trios.
* **Processor** (`HeroRework.AcademyHeroKindPreprocessor` / `AcademyHeroKindProcessor`, runs on every machine): finds the pending choice of that gauge (only if it is still the untouched 3-reference trio - idempotent), collects the candidates of the wanted kind (the trio first, then `Academy.AvailableHeroes`), and takes a **deterministic** pair: `HeroAcademy.PickPair` sorts the names (the pool order in memory must not matter) and shuffles with the game PRNG `Amplitude.Utilities.Random(Galaxy.Seed + Turn*7 + gaugeIndex*131 + empireIndex*1009)` - the same numbers on every machine. Dropped trio heroes go back to the pool (`MakeHeroAvailableAgain`), new candidates leave it (`MakeHeroUnavailable`), `HeroChoice.HeroDefinitionReferences` is replaced with the pair and `EventHeroUnlockGaugeFilled` is raised again, so the notification redraws with two cards (the window draws any number of cards and picks by index). No candidate of the kind → the vanilla trio stays untouched.
* **Toggles.** God-Mod → heroes tab: «Выбор типа героя при заполнении шкалы Академии» (`HeroRework.AcademyOfferEnabled`, session only). The "asked" memory is not persisted: after a reload a still-pending choice is asked about again.
* **Tests:** `tests/HeroAcademyTests.cs` - determinism, pool-order independence, randomness, 0/1/2-candidate edge cases, name-hash stability (run with `Amplitude.dll` next to the exe).

## Admiral class trees: the hero's own ship (2026-10-25, reworked same day after vanilla data check)

The class trees of the two admiral classes are built around the hero's **personal ship**. Mechanism (no new hooks): vanilla `HeroShip.RefreshHeroDescriptors` takes every ship-channel skill descriptor of the hero and adds it **to the HeroShip object itself**, so the `Path` of each modifier resolves **from the ship**: an empty path = the hero's own ship, `./ClassGarrisonFleet` = his fleet, `./ClassGarrisonFleet/ClassShip` = every ship of the fleet. Everything the class tree gives to the fleet travels **through the hero's ship** and disappears with the hero.

**Vanilla magnitudes (checked in `SimulationDescriptors[HeroSkill].xml` of the base game):** a single fleet-damage skill goes up to `ShipDamage +0.40` at level 5 (HeroSkillShip18), and the per-ship variants go twice as high - `Damage +0.80` (HeroSkillShip22_3, HeroSkillShip49_4) and `MaximumShield +0.80` (HeroSkillShip26_3). The user's memory is confirmed: a bonus aimed at a ship is much bigger than a fleet-wide one, up to +40-80% for ONE skill. The Guardian numbers below follow that scale.

* **Guardian (`ClassAdmiral@A`) - "Личный флагман" / Personal Flagship.** The combat line of his own warship: T1 `ShipDmg` +8/10/12% weapon damage per level (level 5 of tier 1 = **+40%**, the vanilla single-skill figure; tier 3 = +60%; property `Damage` - the one vanilla fleet-damage skills scale per ship), T2 `ShipHp` +10/15/20% hull (`MaximumHealth`, Percent; tier 3 level 5 = +100%), T3 `ShipCrit` +2/3/4% critical chance (`CriticalHitChance`, the weapon-module property), ultimate **+100% damage, +150% hull, +25% crit, +100% `ManpowerDamageOverTime`** (crew culling) - all with an empty path.
* **Seeker (`ClassAdventurer@A`) - \"Материнский корабль\" / Mothership.** Utility line, everything through his ship: T1 `Move` +1..3 **and** `Vision` +1..4 (both on `./ClassGarrisonFleet`), T2 `Flak` «Зенитный огонь флота» +2/4..10 flak power - the exact modifier pair of the vanilla Flak Enhancer module, fleet-wide: `FlakEfficiency` on every ship's kinetic guns + `Damage +4/point` on the flak guns (moved up from tier 3 per the 2026-10-25 order; the jammer lost its slot to it and is **library-only** now), T3 two **tech mirrors**: `Stealth` «Маскировка флота» and `Detect` «Сеть обнаружения» (details below); the ultimate is the **Mothership** hull swap - implemented, see below. **Hull repair is removed from the class tree** (it stays in the party/race trees); the reserve crews concept (`Crew`) stays in the library for the stage-3 mothership.
* **Tech mirrors (implemented 2026-10-25):** `Stealth` and `Detect` are 3-level skills **without static descriptors**. The live level = `min(skill level, best researched module level)`: the hero ship carries exactly what the best researched cloaker / anti-cloaker module would give. `HeroMirror` (pure, in `HeroTreeDesign.cs`) holds the source tables - module name + the technology it needs (resolved at runtime from the module's `TechnologyPrerequisite`, with verified fallback names `TechnologyArmyCap1/PlayDeck1/PlayDeck3/PlayDeck5` for stealth and `TechnologyPlayDeck2` for detection level 1; detection II/III have no fallback - their techs are resolved from the modules only) - and `HeroRework` wires it: `AddMirrorSkills` (called from `FilterShip` for every ship hero) checks the empire's researched technologies (`empire.GetSimulationDescriptors()` carries one descriptor per researched tech - exactly what the game's own `TechnologyPrerequisite` checks) and injects the cached live descriptor `HRA_Mirror_<Special>_<level>` (`HeroSkillShip` type, like every ship-skill descriptor). Stealth adds `AntiVisionStrengthForModule +N` on the hero ship (the vanilla cloaker property), detection adds `VisionStrength +N` and, from level 2, `MaximumShipDetectionProbeStock +N` (the vanilla anti-cloaker pair). A new IL hook (end of `DepartmentOfScience.UnlockTechnology`, both overloads) calls `OnTechnologyUnlocked`, which refreshes the descriptors of every active ship hero of the empire - a freshly researched cloaking tech immediately upgrades the skill. The mirror never bypasses the military tree: no tech, no effect (level 0).
* **Mothership hull swap (implemented 2026-10-25):** the learned Seeker ultimate REBUILDS the hero ship as a mothership - no bonus list, a different SHIP. Mechanism (game API only, no new IL hooks): at the next turn pass (`HeroRework.PerTurn`, where the mod already walks every hero) `TryMothership` sees the ultimate, resolves the empire's large carrier hull (`HullLarge01` + `Empire.Faction.Name`, e.g. HullLarge01Terrans; HullLarge01Terrans as a safe fallback - retrofitting does not check build prerequisites), and lazily builds the design: a hidden non-editable `ShipDesignDefinition` in the pattern of the vanilla hero designs (Tags HideUpkeep, MustBePreloaded) on that hull with the **ShipRoleCarrier** role (the carrier hulls carry layouts for it - `GetShipDesignLayoutDefinitionByShipRole` has no fallback, a ShipRoleHero design on a carrier hull would break on save load - and the role itself buffs squadrons by +10% health/damage). The design is created through the game's own path (`DepartmentOfDefense.ServerAddShipDesign` -> registered game entity -> survives save/load together with its layout), then the slots are filled from the pure plan in `HeroMothership`: every squadron-capable slot carries a bomber wing (`ModuleSquadronBomber1`), the first pure support slot the squadron-defense booster, other support slots engines, defense slots alternate shield/plating; large weapon mounts and pure weapon slots stay EMPTY - a flying fleet base, not a battleship. The swap itself is `DepartmentOfDefense.ApplyShipDesignToShip` (the game's hero-ship binding): `Ship.BindShipDesign` rebuilds the sections from the new hull, re-applies the hull/role/layout descriptors, resets properties and fires `EventShipLayoutChanged`. The ship keeps its hero nature: the ultimate's skill descriptor (injected with the mirrors) carries `CommandPoints Force 0` - the exact modifier of ShipRoleHero - so the mothership stays a free 0-CP, no-upkeep command unit; hero skill descriptors are re-applied right after the swap by `RefreshHeroDescriptors`.
* **Rejected:** conversion of a defense slot into an attack slot; 1:1 copies of support modules as default levels (the mirrors are the sanctioned exception - they follow the researched technology); fleet "auras" from the class tree; ShipRoleHero on a carrier hull (breaks save load - the carrier hulls have no hero layout).

**Battle mechanics research (for the later stages, from the current-game data of ESG-Mod and the game assemblies):**
* **EMP exists** as two tier-4 military weapon modules: `ModuleWeaponBeamEMP1Strategic3` (beam, `EMPDurationForShields` +6 s - disables enemy shields) and `ModuleWeaponMissileEMP1Strategic4` (missile, `EMPDurationForWeapons` +7 s - disables enemy weapons; also disables enemy flak). Their effect is carried by battle actions (`WeaponEMP_SalvoHitTarget_DeactivateShield`/`DeactivateWeapons`) attached to the MODULE - a hero skill cannot attach battle actions, so the attack EMP is planned as a real module of the stage-3 hero hull. «Ракеты-обманки против зениток» = this EMP missile (stage 3). The anti-EMP property `EMPFlotillaDefense` (support module) is the descriptor-side cousin, candidate for a later skill.
* **Stealth exists** (Penumbra DLC): support modules `ModuleSupportInvisibilityLevel1/2` (techs `TechnologyArmyCap1`, `TechnologyPlayDeck1`) and the hero-ship-only Level-3 `ModuleSupportInvisibilityAmbush`/`FullCombat` (`TechnologyPlayDeck3/5`). They `Force` the ship property `AntiVisionStrengthForModule` (levels 1/2/3, the best wins by priority); the FLEET property `AntiVisionStrength` (plus `IsInvisible`/`InvisibilityDetected`) drives visibility. Detection is separate: the real anti-cloaker modules are `ModuleSupportInvisibilityDetectionLevel1/2/3` (detection level 1/2/3; the wiki lists probe stock 2/3 and probe cooldown 1/2 from level 2), the `ModuleInvisibilityDetectionProbeLevel*` names are the "Auto Anti-Cloaker" family that is not implemented in the vanilla game. The T4/T5 anti-cloaker techs (Antimatter Channeling / Quadrinix Toroids) also raise the empire's probe detection level (`DetectionProbeVisionStrength` 2/3).
* **The tech mirror is implemented** (see the class-tree section above): `FilterShip`-time injection plus the end hook of `DepartmentOfScience.UnlockTechnology` (IL postfix; the event itself is private, the hook covers both overloads). `RefreshHeroDescriptors` otherwise runs only on skill updates and game load, so without the hook the mirror level would lag behind a freshly researched tech.
* **Flak formulas:** the flak salvo damage equals the flak module's `Damage` (`Salvo_InitEffectiveDamage`), kinetic guns carry `FlakDPSMissile`/`EfficiencyAgainstSquadron`, and squadrons resist flak via `FlakEvasionSquadron` (the `ModuleSupportFlakScrambler` module - a candidate for the stage-3 mothership that carries squadrons).

* **Tests:** `tests/HeroClassTreeTests.cs` - both trees build with the right concepts, thresholds and ultimate titles; the tier-1 weapon damage reaches the vanilla +40% at level 5; every "own ship" modifier has the empty path and the vanilla-proven property; the flak pair and the jammer mechanic are checked property-by-property; the Seeker stage layout is checked (flak at tier 2, mirrors at tier 3, the jammer gone, the mothership-alone ultimate); `HeroMirror.LevelOf` is checked against every research combination (0 / 1 / 2 / 3, runtime module resolution and the fallback names, no fallback for detection II/III); `HeroMothership.ModuleFor` is checked for every slot kind (squadron / large / support / defense / weapon-only / hybrid) plus the CP-forcing modifier of the hull swap; the whole library (447 skills, all roles) is walked for positive values and anchored paths.

The refresh via `OnSkillsUpdated` on level change, acceptance of runtime descriptor copies by `SimulationObject`, `Hero.get_SkillTrees` being the only GUI path, the stage-threshold clamp, already-assigned mismatched heroes staying assigned without effect, the `FailureInfo` tooltip, and that `Hero.Level` is the quantity the user means by "level".
Stage 3: that the dictatorship tags are present on the senate object under every dictatorship government, that skipping `ServerTriggerRebellion` (also its per-system rebellions caused by the empire rebellion) is acceptable, that vanilla dictatorships may have several senators, that `LevelMax` on a cloned rule is honoured by `UpdateLevel`/GUI and that the starting hero level is 1, and the prime-senator-at-start choice of the leader.
Stage 4: that runtime-registered skills, `GuiElement`s and descriptors are accepted by the game (the registration was only exercised against a synthetic database in a plain Mono process, not in the game), that `GuiElement.Clone()` keeps the icons, that paths such as `../ClassEmpire/ClassGarrisonFleet` accept the fleet properties used for senators (`ShipDamage`, `FleetMaximumHealth`, ...), that the senate descriptors reach the senator of the dictator, that `HeroSkillTreeStage.RequiredLevel` is compared with hero-wide spent skill points (it is, in `ComputeAvailableSkills`), and that all magnitudes are balanced.
Follow-ups: that a client-posted `OrderRecruitHero` for a hidden runtime definition is accepted and processed by the game, that `Touch`/`Add` of the definitions before `SerializeElements` is early enough for a load, that the faction names resolve to a faction key, that removing a hero with `RemoveHero` on every client is consistent (no order exists for it), that the senators' representative refresh picks the new hero, and the pace of the passive experience (10 / 6 turns per level) - none of it has been tried in the game.
